# Knowledge source qualification for Fleet

Fleet may consume a public Knowledge revision only by a full 40-character commit SHA that is on the trusted `main` history. The source at that SHA is the artifact: pin the checkout, record the SHA and hash the exact files used in the private publication receipt. The relevant adapter inputs are `references/external/**`, `tools/{validate_external_claim_adapter,test_external_claim_adapter}.py`, and the `specs/002-external-claim-adapter/**` contract.

The public `external-claim-adapter` workflow runs on every `main` push; its stable job is `offline-integrity`. It executes `python tools/test_external_claim_adapter.py` and `python tools/validate_external_claim_adapter.py --root .` on a free GitHub-hosted runner with only `contents: read`. PR validation remains path-scoped to adapter inputs. A Fleet consumer must require the completed successful **push** workflow and job on the *same exact SHA* as its pinned checkout. Missing, pending, skipped, canceled, failed or PR-only runs do not qualify that SHA. For a retry or rollback, Fleet resolves the exact requested SHA again, rechecks main ancestry and the run, and verifies the source digest.

This check covers adapter code and canonical registries offline. It does **not** independently re-resolve every article DOI, citation or scientific claim in the corpus. If a Portfolio release consumes other Knowledge paths such as `corpus/**` or `reports/**`, its private Fleet publication policy must separately identify and validate those inputs and record their SHA/digest; do not call the adapter gate a full-corpus integrity proof. Public Knowledge CI has no Azure OIDC or private Fleet dispatch credential. Fleet #177 owns polling, cross-repository pinning, receipts and protected publication.


For Fleet's current polling cadence, operator retry path, receipt location, and deployment boundary, see the [Fleet workflow and deployed-surface map](https://github.com/pH34r-pH/long-haul-fleet/blob/main/docs/workflow-and-surface-map.md).
